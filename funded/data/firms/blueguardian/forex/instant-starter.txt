- 2nd breach, the account is breached.What is the Maximum Daily Loss?
Max daily loss is based on 3% of the Initial account balance.
We calculate daily drawdown as follows: Upon the 5pm Est day change, we will take the higher figure of either the account balance or account equity and subtract a fixed 3% of the initial balance from the higher value as the daily loss limit for the new day.

Example 1:
On a $5,000 Account, if at 5pm EST you have an open trade with a floating profit of $100, your account equity will be $5,100. With a 3% daily drawdown, the equity cannot drop below $4950 ($5,100 - $150 = $4,950) on the next trading day.

Example 2:
If on a $5,000 Account at 5pm EST you have an open trade with a floating loss of -$100, your account equity will be $4,900. Because your $5,000 balance is higher than your $4,900 equity, your daily drawdown limit of $150 will be calculated on a $5,000 balance. This means for the next trading day, the equity cannot drop below $4,850 ($5,000 - $150 = $4,850).
‍
Example 3:
If there are no open trades at 5pm EST, the daily drawdown will be 3% of initial balance.How does the Max Drawdown work?
Max drawdown will remain at 5% trailing of your Highest Watermark Closing Trade made. If your account reaches 5% of your starting balance your trailing Drawdown will lock in at your starting balance.

Once your Drawdown is locked in at your starting balance we add a fixed 1% buffer on the account. This way you cannot withdraw all the profit and breach your account.

Example 1: You have a 100k account. Your Daily drawdown is a fixed 3% and your Max Trailing is 5%. Your Daily drawdown level is $97,000 and your Max trailing is $95,000. If you make $8,000 profit, once your account has made over 5% of the starting balance your Trailing now locks in at $100,000. It no longer trails. Drawdown includes profits and losses from closed positions, swap fees and commissions

Example 2: You start out with a 100k account. Your Daily drawdown is 3% and your Max Trailing is 5%. Your Daily drawdown level is $97,000 and your Max Trailing is $95,000. Lets say you make $5,000 profit. This puts your account at $105,000. The next day your Daily drawdown will be $105,000- $3000 = $102,000. Your Max trailing drawdown is $105,000-$5,000 = $100,000. Your new Max trailing drawdown is $100,000 because it trails from your highest recorded balance which was $105,000.

Daily drawdown will reset each Day at 5PM EST. Max Trailing will always be from Highest recorded balance unless you achieve 5% from your starting balance.

Consistency Rule
The consistency rule applies to Blue Guardian instant funded starter challenges.

Please make sure to read the below carefully before you start trading.

On instant funded starter challenges a consistency rule of 15% applies, that means that one trading day can not equal or be greater than 15% of the total profits made. When the profits of one trading day equals or is greater than 15% of total profits made that payout period, you CANNOT request a payout until the highest profit trading day falls below the 15% of total profits made on that account within that particular period.

What if I make more than 15% of my total PnL in one day? Will that breach my account? Your account will not be terminated if you violate the 15% rule, you must continue trading and make more profits until the highest profit trading day falls below the 15% of total profits.
